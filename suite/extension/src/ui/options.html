Settings

Switch each helper on or off. Everything is off until you turn it on. Your choices stay on this computer.

Agent connection (advanced)

Practice mode runs entirely on this computer and needs nothing here. Later, the Agent may use a server of ours so that no secret key is ever stored in your browser. This is off, and nothing uses it yet.

Clear everything

Erases everything this extension keeps on this computer: your switches, the practice assistant's memory, its waiting approvals, saved background jobs and reports, any saved lists, and the history of what the assistant did (its audit trail). This cannot be undone. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
