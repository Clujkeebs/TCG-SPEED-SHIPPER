TCG Speed Shipper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Short version: TCG Speed Shipper processes everything locally in your browser. No CSV data, buyer addresses, or order information is ever sent to any server. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

What This Site Does

TCG Speed Shipper is a free, client-side web tool that converts TCGPlayer packing list CSV files into printable PDF shipping labels. The tool runs entirely in your web browser using JavaScript.

When you upload a CSV file, it is read by your browser's built-in FileReader API and processed locally. The resulting PDF is generated in your browser using the jsPDF library and downloaded directly to your device. At no point does your CSV file — or any data it contains — leave your computer.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • CSV file contents or buyer order data
  • Buyer names or shipping addresses from your packing list
  • Your return address or store name
  • Any personally identifiable information from your orders

Your return address is saved to your own browser's localStorage — a private storage area on your device that only your browser can access. It never leaves your machine.

Cookies

TCG Speed Shipper does not set any cookies.

Third-Party Services

This site loads resources from the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to load the fonts used in the site design. Google's font CDN may log your IP address per their privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare CDN — the jsPDF library is loaded from a Cloudflare-hosted CDN. Cloudflare may log standard HTTP request data.

Children's Privacy

TCG Speed Shipper is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since this tool collects no personal data at all, there is nothing to disclose under COPPA or similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date. Because we collect no personal data, changes are unlikely to affect your privacy in any meaningful way.
