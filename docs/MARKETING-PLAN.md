## The five channels, ranked by expected return per hour

### 1. Partner / referral outreach (creators only; shop cold email paused)
- **2026-09-28:** an older campaign (Sep 13–25) had already emailed about
  200 shops and creators with 0 human replies. Today 8 new creators got
  personalized 40% offers. They get one follow-up on Oct 5, then we stop.
  Devin finds 10 more creators a week; no more shops.
- **22 personalized emails are written and queued** in
  `docs/outreach/README.md`: 10 creators and 12 shops, all public business
  contacts Devin verified at the source.
- **Offer:**
  - **Creators:** 40% recurring commission, a free year of Premium, and
    their viewers get a free first month.
  - **Shops:** the free tool, plus the same partner link for their
    Discord or regulars.
- **Send plan:** 8 a day over 3 days, best-fit first, from the owner's
  Gmail. One email per person, no follow-up for 7 days, then one short
  follow-up, then stop.
- **Who:** Claude sends once the address is on file. Replies go to the
  owner; Claude drafts answers and sets up partner links.
- **Success:** 3 partners live, 1 video mention.

### 3. Weekly newsletter (retention + trust)
- Every Tuesday 7:52am PT the routine sends the issue. Issues 1 (Sep 29)
  and 2 (Oct 6) are written and fact-checked. Devin writes issue 3 (Oct 13)
  by the Monday before.
- **List growth (shipped today):**
  - The "labels ready" popup now asks "Get one shipping tip every Tuesday".
    That's the moment sellers are happiest with us.
  - The footer form stays on every page.
  - Every blog post ends by pointing at the newsletter.
- **Target:** 40 subscribers. Every issue links one feature with a UTM tag,
  so we can see which issues bring people back.

### 4. Short-form video (owner's face = trust)
- A 60-second screen demo: CSV → drop → print. Devin writes the
  shot-by-shot script; the owner records it once.
- Post it to TikTok, Shorts, Reels and X; the new X/OG card makes shared
  links look sharp. Then one short a week: a tip from that week's
  newsletter.
- The same clip goes into every partner reply ("here's what your viewers
  would see").

### 5. Communities (slow, but exactly our buyers)
- One helpful post a week from the owner's own account, following
  `docs/community-channels.md`.
- Start with the Unpack Hits TCG Seller Club (Skool) and the TCGCSV
  Discord. Answer the question first, and disclose "I built this".
- Agents never post as the owner.
