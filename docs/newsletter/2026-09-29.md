**1. Tip: price the whole envelope, not the card**

USPS prices a stamped letter by the finished envelope: card, sleeve,
toploader, slip and envelope together. From the schedule effective Oct 4,
2026 (USPS Notice 123):

- Up to 1 oz: $0.82, which is one Forever stamp
- Up to 2 oz: $1.11
- Up to 3 oz: $1.40
- Up to 3.5 oz: $1.69. Over 3.5 oz, it isn't a letter anymore.

A stiff or lumpy envelope, like a toploader that doesn't bend, can be
"nonmachinable", which adds $0.49 (USPS DMM 101). Weigh one packed envelope
of each kind you send, once, and you'll know your numbers.
Sources: https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/Notice123.htm ·
https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/101.htm
