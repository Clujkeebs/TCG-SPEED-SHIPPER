**1. Tip: use a snug sleeve for Yu-Gi-Oh! cards**

A Yu-Gi-Oh! card is 59 × 86 mm, a little smaller than the 63 × 88 mm Magic
and Pokémon size. Sleeves sold as "small" or "Japanese" size fit it snugly.
A standard sleeve technically holds the card but lets it shift, and shifting
wears the corners. So: small sleeve, then a toploader, then a gentle shake.
If anything moves, add a cardboard stiffener behind it before the envelope.

Source: https://www.tcgstacked.com/yugioh/guides/sleeves-and-sizes
