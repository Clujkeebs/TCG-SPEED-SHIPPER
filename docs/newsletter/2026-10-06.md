Subject: A buyer says their order never came. Now what?
Preview: TCGplayer's missing-package rules in 5 bullets, plus labels and slips on one page.

Hi,

**1. Tip: the missing-package playbook**

Sooner or later a buyer writes "it never came." TCGplayer's help center
spells out what you're expected to do:

- **Answer within 2 business days** (Mon–Fri, not holidays). If you don't,
  the buyer can escalate to TCGplayer Customer Service.
- **Reported on the estimated delivery date (EDD) or within 2 business days
  after it?** You can ask them to wait until the EDD + 2 business days.
  Reported later than that? Refund right away. Expedited orders get the same
  timing.
- **Refunding:** on the order page choose Full Refund, then Refund Origin
  "Buyer Initiated" and Refund Reason "Package - Missing".
- **Untracked envelope that didn't arrive by the EDD:** it's a full refund.
  New: US sellers with 1,000+ lifetime orders can get that refund credited
  back through TCGplayer Safeguard on orders placed on or after Aug 17, 2026.
  Refund first, then escalate the message thread or open a ticket.
- **Over $49.99:** the order needs tracking or delivery confirmation (USPS,
  UPS or FedEx) for seller protection.
