Subject: Package postage just went up (stamps didn't)
Preview: USPS holiday prices on packages through Jan 17, the Dec 17 cutoff, and a faster thermal setup.

Hi,

**1. Tip: send envelopes, not packages, when you can**

USPS's temporary holiday price increase is on now, running Oct 4, 2026 to
Jan 17, 2027. It averages about 6% and only covers package services:
Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express and Parcel Select.
Commercial Ground Advantage goes up $0.40 to $3.15 a package in zones 1–4,
and $0.55 to $7.70 in zones 5–9.

**Stamped letters aren't part of it.** A plain white envelope is still $0.82
for the first ounce, plus $0.29 for each extra ounce. So for the next three
months, every order under about $50 that fits safely in a toploader and
envelope saves you more than usual. Keep packages for orders that need
tracking (TCGplayer requires it over $49.99) or that won't fit flat.

Sources: https://www.retaildive.com/news/usps-increase-2026-peak-season-holidays/828858/ ·
https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/Notice123.htm

How to pack a PWE so it stays machinable:

**2. What changed: the date to circle, and a new game**

- **Dec 17** is USPS's last recommended day to mail First-Class Mail (your
  stamped envelopes) and Ground Advantage for delivery by Dec 25 in the lower
  48. Priority Mail is Dec 18. Military and international dates come out in
  mid-October.
  Source: https://about.usps.com/newsroom/national-releases/2026/0922-usps-recommends-2026-holiday-mailing-shipping-dates.htm
- **NARUTO CARD GAME is now on TCGplayer.** Sellers could list from Sept 22
  and buyers could buy from Sept 28. For now that means promo cards from
  events like Gen Con; the first official set is planned for summer 2027.
  Source: https://seller.tcgplayer.com/blog/now-available-on-tcgplayer-naruto-card-game

Add TCGplayer's Packing Slip PDF next to your shipping export and pick the
4×6 format. Each order's slip prints on its own 4×6 right behind its label,
so you peel, stick, tuck and move on. No sorting a stack of slips at the end.
