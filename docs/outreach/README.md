## Findings (2026-09-28): read before sending anything

- **An earlier campaign already cold-emailed about 200 shops and creators**
  (Sep 13–25), offering "30% revenue share". It got **0 human replies**:
  only auto-responders (Galactic Toys, Card Kingdom ticket, Backyard
  Breaks) and 6 bounces.
- **Decision: no more cold email to shops.** They get a free-tool pitch
  from strangers all day. Creators get a personalized pitch with a real
  earning reason. Shops come in through SEO, the newsletter and partners
  instead.
- **Before any send**, search Gmail `in:sent to:<address>`. Never email
  someone twice without a reply, except for the single 7-day creator
  follow-up.

## Rules (PERMISSIONS.md, email row)

- One email per person, never CC/BCC. Plain text, no tracking pixels, and
  real links (no redirect wrappers).
- The footer always has why they're getting it, a one-line opt-out ("reply
  'no thanks'"), and the mailing address.
- A "no thanks", "stop" or "unsubscribe" reply means we never email them
  again. Log it in the Status column.
- **Follow-up:** one short nudge after 7 days with no reply, then stop.
- Replies go to the owner's inbox. Claude drafts each answer, and for a
  "yes" sets up their partner link and dashboard the same day.
