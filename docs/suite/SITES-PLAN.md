# Sites plan: one landing site per product

Draft, 2026-10-15. Nothing is bought or built yet. $0 spend until the owner picks domains.

## The idea
Each product gets one small, fast site so it can be found, explained and sold on its own. The Suite site ties them together. All four are static pages with no trackers, built the same way as the Shipper site (privacy first: no third-party scripts).

## Domains (checked 2026-10-15, a read-only lookup, nothing reserved)
| Site | First choice | Also free |
|---|---|---|
| TCG Speed Shipper | tcgspeedshipper.com (owned, live) | none needed |
| TCG Speed Scan | tcgspeedscan.com (free) | tcgspeedscan.app (free) |
| TCG Speed Agent | tcgspeedagent.com (free) | tcgspeedagent.app (free) |
| TCG Speed Suite | tcgspeedsuite.com (free) | tcgspeedsuite.app (free) |

Availability changes any day. Buying all three .com names is the only owner step that costs money, so it needs the owner's yes.

## Pages per site
**Shipper (exists).** Home (upload first), Pricing, Support, Blog, Newsletter, Partners, Terms, Privacy. Add: a "Suite" strip that links to the other products once they exist.

**Scan.** Home (30-second demo and "how it works" in 3 steps), Supported games and accuracy (real numbers from `SCAN-EVAL.md`, nothing invented), Pricing, How the CSV works with TCGplayer, Privacy (photos stay on the phone unless the seller saves them), Join the beta (email box), Support.

**Agent.** Home (what it does for a seller, with three real examples), What it can and cannot do (permission levels, spending limits, audit log), Pricing and limits, Privacy and security, Join the waitlist, Support. Held until the Agent has a working base.

**Suite.** Home (the whole day: ship, list, run), One page per product (links out), Pricing bundle (only once prices are decided), About (a TCGplayer seller built this), Blog (shared with Shipper), Contact.

## Shared look and rules
One logo family and one colour set across the four sites, so they read as one toolkit. No claims we cannot back up (accuracy, savings, "official"). No TCGplayer branding that implies endorsement. Every page works on a phone and an iPad.

## What each needs from the owner
- **Shipper:** nothing.
- **Scan:** a yes to buy the domain; about 50 card photos (board task S5) so the accuracy numbers on the site are real; a short beta list (task S3).
- **Agent:** a yes to buy the domain, and the wait until Scan and the extension have a base.
- **Suite:** a yes to buy the domain; decide whether the bundle price is shown at launch.
- **All:** pick the names (the first choices above are the proposal); say when to start. Claude builds each site once its product works, using the "Suite landing pages and brand" idea on the board.

## Order
1. Suite and Scan pages first (a waitlist can start before the product is finished).
2. Agent page when A1 is working.
3. Cross-links from the Shipper site last, so the live site is not changed early.
