# TCG Speed Scan: try it (for sellers testing early)

**What it is.** A page on your phone or iPad. Point the camera at a card, or pick photos of cards. It works out which card it
is, adds it to a list with a price, and at the end gives you one file to upload to TCGplayer so the cards get listed.

**Be aware (plain words).**
- This is an early test. It can be wrong. You always see the list before anything is downloaded, and nothing is sent to TCGplayer for you.
- The file layout for TCGplayer has not been checked against a real TCGplayer file yet. Look at the first lines of the file before you upload it.
- For now the card list inside the page is small (a sample). If a card is missing, tell us which one.
- Your photos stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us.
- **What is sent out:** looking up a card sends what you type, or the text the camera read from the card, to Scryfall, the Pokémon TCG API and YGOPRODeck (free card sites). Never the photo. You can turn this off with the tickbox on the first screen, but then only the small sample list works.
- If something goes wrong, tap **Report a problem**. It copies a short note (what you typed, which site, the error, the time). Paste it where we told you. It has no photos or names.

**On an iPad or iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open the link in **Safari** (not inside another app). It must start with https.
2. Tap **Check my device**. Everything should show a green tick. Red lines tell you in one sentence what is wrong.
3. Tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open it from the new icon: it opens full screen like an app.
4. The first time you tap **Start live camera**, Safari asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow**. If you tapped Don't Allow by mistake, tap the "aA" in the address bar, then Website Settings, then Camera, then Allow.
5. Keep the iPad flat or leaning, with the card filling most of the picture and good light. The screen stays on while the camera is on.
6. When a new version is ready, a bar at the top says so. Tap **Tap to refresh**.

**Five minutes:**
1. Open the link we sent you. Allow the camera when it asks. (You can also use **Upload photos**.)
2. On the first screen pick your **price rule** (for example "5% under market"), the **condition** and whether the cards are **foil**.
3. Tap **Start live camera**. Hold one card flat and still, filling most of the picture. It beeps or buzzes when it has the card. If it is unsure it shows a few choices: tap the right one.
4. Made a mistake? Open the **List** tab and tap **Undo** or **Remove**. Change a price by typing in the box.
5. Tap **Download CSV**. Open it and check a few rows. Upload it in your TCGplayer seller portal the way you normally import.

**Several lists.** On the **List** tab you can keep more than one list (for example one per box), copy a list, download a file for any list, and open a file you downloaded earlier to carry on. Tick rows to change condition, foil or price for many cards at once; **Undo** puts it back.

**Please tell us (short answers are perfect):**
1. How many cards did you scan, and how long did it take?
2. How many did it get right without you touching anything? How many needed a tap? How many failed?
3. Which cards failed (name, game, and was it foil, sleeved, or in bad light)?
4. Were the prices what you would have set? If not, what rule would you use?
5. Did TCGplayer accept the file? What did it say?
6. Anything slow, confusing or annoying? What did you wish a button did?
7. Would you use this every week? Would you pay for it, and roughly how much per month?
8. Can you send us a photo or two of cards it got wrong? (Optional.)

**About the file (please read).** On the **List** tab, under **File to download**, you pick what the file does:
- **Add these cards to my stock** (the default): your stock goes UP by the cards you scanned. Safe.
- **Set my stock to exactly these counts**: careful, this can LOWER your stock. If you own 20 of a card and the file says 1, TCGplayer may change 20 to 1.
- **Mana Pool file**: for Mana Pool's importer.

**The first time you import any new file, use just 3 cards, into Staged inventory, and check them before anything goes live.** The column names come from TCGplayer's and Mana Pool's help articles (read through a search tool); we have not yet checked the file against a real TCGplayer file. The file uses the same ID, quantity and price columns that Mana Pool's importer says it reads from TCGplayer files.

If you can, tap **Share my results** on the List tab after a session and paste what it copies. It holds no photos, names or prices, only what the camera read and what card you ended up with, and it helps us fix the reading.

Send answers to the person who gave you the link. Thank you.
