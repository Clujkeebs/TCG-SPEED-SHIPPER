# TCG Speed Scan: try it (for sellers testing early)

**What it is.** A page on your phone or iPad. Point the camera at a card, or pick photos of cards. It works out which card it
is, adds it to a list with a price, and at the end gives you one file to upload to TCGplayer so the cards get listed.

**Be aware (plain words).**
- This is an early test. It can be wrong. You always see the list before anything is downloaded, and nothing is sent to TCGplayer for you.
- The file layout for TCGplayer has not been checked against a real TCGplayer file yet. Look at the first lines of the file before you upload it.
- For now the card list inside the page is small (a sample). If a card is missing, tell us which one.
- Your photos stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us.

**Five minutes:**
1. Open the link we sent you. Allow the camera when it asks. (You can also use **Upload photos**.)
2. On the first screen pick your **price rule** (for example "5% under market"), the **condition** and whether the cards are **foil**.
3. Tap **Start live camera**. Hold one card flat and still, filling most of the picture. It beeps or buzzes when it has the card. If it is unsure it shows a few choices: tap the right one.
4. Made a mistake? Open the **List** tab and tap **Undo** or **Remove**. Change a price by typing in the box.
5. Tap **Download CSV**. Open it and check a few rows. Upload it in your TCGplayer seller portal the way you normally import.

**Please tell us (short answers are perfect):**
1. How many cards did you scan, and how long did it take?
2. How many did it get right without you touching anything? How many needed a tap? How many failed?
3. Which cards failed (name, game, and was it foil, sleeved, or in bad light)?
4. Were the prices what you would have set? If not, what rule would you use?
5. Did TCGplayer accept the file? What did it say?
6. Anything slow, confusing or annoying? What did you wish a button did?
7. Would you use this every week? Would you pay for it, and roughly how much per month?
8. Can you send us a photo or two of cards it got wrong? (Optional.)

Send answers to the person who gave you the link. Thank you.
