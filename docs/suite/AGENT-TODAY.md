# The Agent today: what it can do and what it cannot

It works on practice data only. It is not connected to your real shop yet.

## What it can do
- **Answer shop questions:** who your repeat buyers are, where and how often you ship, what is running low, what sold last week.
- **Suggest prices** and spot cards far above or below market.
- **Read a Seller Portal export** you drop in, and give you the five most useful things to fix.
- **Write reply drafts** to buyers, in a tone you pick, with the matching TCGplayer rule noted for you. Rules change, so it always says to check TCGplayer's current article.
- **Remember things about your shop**, which you can see, edit and delete.
- **Run reports on a schedule**, and suggest five things worth doing each week.

## Three levels, and when it asks you
1. **Looks.** It only reads. It runs without asking. *Example: "Who are my repeat buyers?"*
2. **Drafts.** It writes something for you but never sends it. *Example: a reply to a buyer whose card arrived bent. You read it and send it yourself.*
3. **Changes.** It asks first, every time, with a card showing what, why and how to undo it. *Example: "Lower 12 foil prices." Nothing changes until you tap Approve.*

Scheduled jobs ask the same way. Only you can let one act alone; none does.

## What it cannot do
- Send a message, give a refund, buy anything or touch TCGplayer.
- Raise its own limits or turn off the Stop button.
- Be given orders by a buyer's message or a file. It treats that text as information only.

## Stop
The Stop button always works.
