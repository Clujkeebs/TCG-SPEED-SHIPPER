# The Agent today: what it can do and what it cannot

It works on practice data only. It is not connected to your real shop yet.

## What it can do
- **Read your order file** and tell you: repeat buyers, top states, busiest days, average order, and how many orders need tracking or a signature. It handles thousands of orders in a fraction of a second.
- **Give you a morning briefing** in under 150 words.
- **Answer shop questions** and **suggest prices**.
- **Read a Seller Portal export** and name the five best things to fix.
- **Write reply drafts in your own voice**, learned from three or four of your past replies, with the matching TCGplayer rule noted for you. Rules change, so check TCGplayer's current article.
- **Remember things about your shop.** You can see, edit and delete them. They survive closing the app.
- **Run reports on a schedule** and suggest five things worth doing each week.

## Three levels, and when it asks you
1. **Looks.** It only reads, so it just does it. *Example: "Who are my repeat buyers?"*
2. **Drafts.** It writes something for you but never sends it. *Example: a reply to a buyer whose card arrived bent. You send it yourself.*
3. **Changes.** It asks first, every time, with a card showing what, why and how to undo. *Example: "Lower 12 foil prices." Nothing changes until you tap Approve.*

## What it cannot do
- Send a message, refund, buy anything or touch TCGplayer.
- Raise its own limits or turn off Stop.
- Take orders from a buyer's message or a file. That text is information only.
