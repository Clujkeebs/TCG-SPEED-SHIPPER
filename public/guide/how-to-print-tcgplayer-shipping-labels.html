How to Print TCGPlayer Shipping Labels Fast

The whole process, from exporting your packing list to a stack of print-ready labels, takes under five minutes and doesn't require an account for the first 10 labels a month.

Updated September 2026 · 4 min read

Step 1: Export your packing list from TCGPlayer

1

Log in to your TCGPlayer seller account and go to Orders in your seller dashboard.

2

Click Packing List, then look for the Export button.

3

TCGPlayer generates a CSV file containing every order that hasn't been marked shipped yet. Save it somewhere you'll find it — Downloads is fine.

Step 2: Enter your return address once

Open TCG Speed Shipper and enter your return address. It's saved in your browser's local storage, not on a server, so it's there automatically next time — you only ever type it once per device.

Step 3: Upload the CSV file

Drag the exported CSV onto the upload area, or click to browse for it. The file is read and parsed entirely in your browser using the FileReader API — buyer names, addresses, and order contents never leave your device. If the file doesn't parse, see the troubleshooting note below or the full CSV format explainer.

Step 4: Choose your label format

Pick 4×6 if you have a thermal label printer (Rollo, Dymo, MUNBYN, and similar all use this size), or 8.5×11 with 1, 2, 3, or 4 labels per page if you're printing on a regular home printer. Toggle packing slips on if you want a matching slip generated after each label.

Step 5: Download and print

Click Download to generate the PDF. When you print it, choose Actual Size or 100% scale in your print dialog — never "Fit to Page," which rescales the label and throws off the dimensions. For a 4×6 thermal printer, also confirm the paper size is set to exactly 4 × 6 inches in the printer driver.

CSV didn't parse? The most common cause is TCGPlayer changing an export column name, or a malformed first export in a session. Try re-exporting once — the second export is usually clean. For the full breakdown of what's actually in the file, see TCGPlayer Packing List CSV Format Explained.

Don't have a thermal printer yet?

You don't need one to start — the 8.5×11 format works with any printer you already own. Once volume picks up, see our Rollo vs. Dymo vs. MUNBYN comparison for which thermal printer fits your order volume and budget.
