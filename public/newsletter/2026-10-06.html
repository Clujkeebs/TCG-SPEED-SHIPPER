A buyer says their order never came. Now what?

TCG seller weekly · October 6, 2026 · Get it every Tuesday

Hi,

1. Tip: the missing-package playbook

Sooner or later a buyer writes "it never came." TCGplayer's help center spells out what you're expected to do:

  • Answer within 2 business days (Mon–Fri, not holidays). If you don't, the buyer can escalate to TCGplayer Customer Service.
  • Reported on the estimated delivery date (EDD) or within 2 business days after it? You can ask them to wait until the EDD + 2 business days. Reported later than that? Refund right away. Expedited orders get the same timing.
  • Refunding: on the order page choose Full Refund, then Refund Origin "Buyer Initiated" and Refund Reason "Package - Missing".
  • Untracked envelope that didn't arrive by the EDD: it's a full refund. New: US sellers with 1,000+ lifetime orders can get that refund credited back through TCGplayer Safeguard on orders placed on or after Aug 17, 2026. Refund first, then escalate the message thread or open a ticket.
  • Over $49.99: the order needs tracking or delivery confirmation (USPS, UPS or FedEx) for seller protection.

Sources: help.tcgplayer.com/hc/en-us/articles/201318116-How-do-I-resolve-missing-orders-packages-for-my-customers · help.tcgplayer.com/hc/en-us/articles/24768427937943-Seller-Guide-to-Refunds-and-Returns

The full walkthrough, including "marked delivered but not received": tcgspeedshipper.com/blog/tcgplayer-lost-package.html

2. What changed: new stamp prices are live (Oct 4)

The USPS letter prices from last week's issue are now in effect: $0.82 for the first ounce, $0.29 for each extra ounce, up to 3.5 oz, plus $0.49 if the envelope is nonmachinable. Old Forever stamps still cover the first ounce. Source: pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/Notice123.htm
