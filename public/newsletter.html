The TCG seller weekly

Every Tuesday · free · written by a TCGplayer seller

One short email a week, readable in two minutes. A shipping tip you can use that day, what changed at TCGplayer and USPS, and the release dates that will hit your order queue. Every fact links to its source.

What's in an issue

  • 1 shipping tip: for example, what a stamped envelope with a toploader really costs under the Oct 4, 2026 USPS prices, and when it turns "nonmachinable".
  • What changed: TCGplayer policy updates (missing packages, returns, tracking thresholds), USPS price changes, and the holiday mailing deadlines.
  • What's coming: the set releases that drive order spikes, so you can stock envelopes and toploaders ahead of them.
  • 1 time-saver: one thing in TCG Speed Shipper that fits that week's tip.

The promises

  • Weekly, never more. No ads for other companies.
  • One-click unsubscribe in every email. Reply "stop" and it works too.
  • Your email is only used for this newsletter. It's never sold or shared (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Having a TCG Speed Shipper account doesn't subscribe you. You only get it if you sign up here.
