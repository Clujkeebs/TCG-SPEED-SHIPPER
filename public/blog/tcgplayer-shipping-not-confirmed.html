"Shipping Not Confirmed" on TCGplayer: What It Means

It doesn't mean your cards are lost. It means one button hasn't been clicked yet. Here's what to do about it, from either side of the order.

Published September 26, 2026 · 5 min read

"Shipping not confirmed" is one of the most-searched TCGplayer statuses, and it confuses buyers and sellers for opposite reasons. Buyers worry their cards were never sent. Sellers sometimes don't realize their orders are showing it at all. Here's what it actually means on both sides of the order.

What the status means

It means exactly what it says: the seller hasn't yet marked the order as shipped in TCGplayer's system. It says nothing about whether the package physically went out. Plenty of orders sitting in the mail right now show "not confirmed" because a seller dropped a stack of envelopes in the mailbox and hasn't clicked the button yet.

For PWE (plain white envelope) orders, which have no tracking at all, confirming shipment is the only signal the buyer gets. See TCGplayer Order Tracking Explained for why PWE orders never show scan updates.

If you're the buyer

  • Give it until the ship-by date. Sellers have a handling window; an order placed Friday night may not be confirmed until the next business day or two.
  • Message the seller through TCGplayer if it's past the ship-by date. Most sellers answer quickly, and many will say "it went out, I forgot to confirm."
  • Contact TCGplayer support if the seller doesn't respond in a reasonable time. TCGplayer can step in on orders that never ship.

If you're the seller

Unconfirmed orders look like unshipped orders to buyers and to TCGplayer. That drives anxious messages, cancellation requests, and — if it happens past the ship-by date — marks against the on-time shipping that feeds your seller level.

1

Confirm the same day you ship. Make it the last step of your shipping session, not something you'll do later.

2

Add tracking where you have it. For tracked orders, entering the tracking number with the right carrier confirms shipment and protects you in a "never arrived" dispute.

3

Keep a checklist for big batches. When you're packing 40 orders, it's easy to lose track of which ones actually went in the tub.

A checklist that's already built. TCG Speed Shipper puts a "shipped" checkbox on every order in your batch, remembers your progress if you close the tab, and can print only the orders you haven't marked yet. Paste your tracking numbers in and you get a one-click copy list to enter each one back into TCGplayer — the fastest way to go from "not confirmed" to confirmed for a whole batch.

"Not confirmed" vs. "no tracking"

These get mixed up constantly. Not confirmed means the seller hasn't clicked "shipped." No tracking available on a confirmed order almost always means it went out as a PWE, which has no tracking by design. Neither one, on its own, means the package is lost.

When to worry

A buyer should start asking questions when an order is still unconfirmed well past its ship-by date, or confirmed but not arrived after the estimated delivery date. A seller should worry when orders pile up unconfirmed — that's a process problem worth fixing before it becomes an account problem. See How to Avoid Getting Suspended as a TCGplayer Seller.

FAQ

What does shipping not confirmed mean on TCGplayer?

The seller hasn't marked the order as shipped in TCGplayer yet. It doesn't necessarily mean the package hasn't been mailed.

What should I do if my TCGplayer order says shipping not confirmed?

Wait until the ship-by date, then message the seller through TCGplayer. If the seller doesn't respond in a reasonable time, contact TCGplayer support.

How do sellers avoid shipping not confirmed?

Mark orders shipped the same day they go out, and enter tracking numbers with the correct carrier for tracked orders.
