How to Ship Baseball Cards

Raw rookie, graded slab, or a box of commons: the packing changes with each. Here's how to ship every kind of baseball card safely without overpaying.

Published September 27, 2026 · 6 min read

Baseball and other sports cards ship the same way as any trading card, with one difference: more of them are graded, and slabs need their own packing. Here's how to handle each type.

Raw (ungraded) cards

1

Penny sleeve, then a toploader (or a card saver for anything you expect the buyer to send for grading: graders prefer semi-rigid holders).

2

Seal the top with a team bag or a small strip of painter's tape so the card can't slide out.

3

Stiffen and envelope it. For low-value cards, sandwich the toploader between thin cardboard in a plain white envelope. For anything more valuable, use a bubble mailer with tracking.

Graded slabs (PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC)

  • Put the slab in a slab sleeve or team bag to protect the case from scratches.
  • Wrap it in two or three layers of bubble wrap.
  • Ship in a padded mailer for a single inexpensive slab, or a small box with void fill for anything valuable or for multiple slabs. The slab must not rattle.
  • Always use tracking. For high-value slabs, add signature confirmation and insurance.

For a detailed slab-only packing walkthrough, see How to Ship Graded Cards.

Envelope, eBay Standard Envelope, or tracked mailer?

A plain white envelope with a stamp is the cheapest option but has no tracking. If you sell on eBay, eBay Standard Envelope is a low-cost option with tracking for raw cards in a letter-size envelope, for lower-priced orders; eBay sets the value limit and size rules, so check its current requirements. Everything else, including every slab, should go in a bubble mailer or box with USPS Ground Advantage (tracked) or a faster service.

For a full cost comparison, see How Much Does It Cost to Ship Trading Cards?

Lots and team sets

Group cards in sleeves, then in a team bag or a small card box, and ship in a box with padding so they can't shift. Big lots are often cheaper to ship by weight with Ground Advantage than you'd expect.

Mistakes that cause damage claims

  • Shipping a slab in an envelope: slab corners break through, and the case can crack.
  • A toploader with nothing stiffening it in a PWE.
  • Not insuring a card you couldn't afford to refund.
  • No tracking on a valuable order, so you can't prove delivery.

FAQ

Can you ship baseball cards in a regular envelope?

Yes, for low-value raw cards: sleeve, toploader, cardboard for stiffness, and a stamp. There's no tracking, and a rigid envelope may be charged the USPS nonmachinable surcharge.

How do you ship a graded card?

Put the slab in a sleeve, wrap it in bubble wrap, and ship it in a padded mailer or box with tracking. Add signature confirmation and insurance for high-value slabs.

Can I ship baseball cards Media Mail?

No. Trading cards aren't eligible for USPS Media Mail. See Can You Ship Trading Cards Media Mail?

What's the cheapest tracked way to ship a card?

On eBay, eBay Standard Envelope is usually cheapest for eligible raw cards. Elsewhere, a bubble mailer with USPS Ground Advantage is the usual choice.
