Does TCGplayer Provide Shipping Labels?

The Seller Portal gives Marketplace sellers order and shipping tools. Postage is a separate job—and Direct fulfillment is a different workflow.

Published September 27, 2026 · 7 min read

No. For a regular seller-fulfilled TCGplayer Marketplace order, TCGplayer provides order details and tools to record shipment, but sellers buy postage separately through a carrier or shipping platform. TCGplayer's workflow is to export order information, prepare postage elsewhere, then upload tracking when applicable. TCG Speed Shipper turns the export into address labels, envelopes, and packing slips; it does not buy postage or create a prepaid carrier label.

The short answer: order data, not postage

Think of shipping as two separate jobs. First, get the buyer's name and address onto an envelope or package. Second, pay a carrier or postage provider for the service that will carry it. An address label solves the first job; it does not pay for the second.

TCGplayer's export guide directs Marketplace sellers to download shipping information as CSV and import it into a shipping platform of their choice. Its tracking guide describes uploading tracking data from that postage provider back into Seller Portal. The Portal manages order data; postage is bought elsewhere.

Address labels and postage labels are different

A printed address label is the recipient's name and delivery address in a convenient format. It can save you from handwriting or retyping every order. A postage label is issued through a carrier or postage service after you select and pay for a mail class or service. Depending on the service, that label may also carry a barcode and tracking information.

For a plain white envelope (PWE), Speed Shipper can print an address on an Avery 5160 sheet or directly on a #10 envelope. Add the appropriate stamp or other postage separately; printer output itself is not paid postage.

For a tracked package, buy postage through the carrier or shipping platform you choose. TCGplayer's export guide names USPS, Stamps.com, FedEx, and Endicia as options for using its shipping data. Once you have tracking, send that number back to the TCGplayer order. Follow current instructions from your carrier or postage provider; no prices are listed here.

What the Seller Portal gives you

The Orders tab supports picking and packing as well as order management. Select orders to print pull sheets or packing slips, or export shipping information; the order list can also be exported to CSV. An individual order page shows its address, shipping speed, and printable packing slip. These tools organize the work; they do not buy postage.

After buying postage, return tracking by keeping the TCGplayer Order Number with the tracking data and using Orders → Import Tracking. TCGplayer applies valid numbers to matching orders and updates status. Untracked orders have separate mark-as-shipped controls.

Does the buyer's package need a packing slip? The official Marketplace help pages describe how to print one, but they do not say that every seller-fulfilled Marketplace package must contain one. Direct reimbursement shipments are a separate case: the Direct Seller Agreement requires sellers to include the relevant portion of the RI packing slip when sending those replenishment cards to TCGplayer.

Direct is fulfillment; Pro is a toolset

TCGplayer Direct changes who ships the buyer's order. TCGplayer says it fulfills eligible Direct orders on the seller's behalf, then sends the seller a Reimbursement Invoice (RI) for the cards sold. Instead of mailing each Direct buyer's order yourself, you send the listed inventory to TCGplayer to replenish its stock.

“Pro” is not another name for that shipping service. TCGplayer describes Pro as inventory, pricing, and store tools, and lists Direct and Store Your Products separately as fulfillment solutions. Marketplace sellers still handle their postage; with Direct, TCGplayer ships eligible buyer orders and the seller later sends RI replenishment inventory.

Check the channel and status in Seller Portal to confirm which workflow applies. TCGplayer's Direct order guide explains how to identify Direct orders and handle RIs.

Where TCG Speed Shipper fits

TCG Speed Shipper is the address-and-packing step between the TCGplayer CSV and the mailpiece. It reads the exported order data and can make Avery 5160 address labels, 4×6 thermal-format address labels, printed envelope layouts, and matching packing slips. Its shipping plan sorts orders into envelope, tracking, and signature tiers so you can review the batch before printing.

That tier view is a workflow aid, not a postage purchase. The app does not sell stamps, charge for carrier service, or produce a carrier-paid tracking barcode. For tracked orders, buy postage separately and use the shipping provider's own prepaid label. For an envelope, print the address and add postage appropriate to the mailing method you choose. For exact requirements, check TCGplayer's current shipping guidance.

A simple CSV-to-shipped workflow

1

Select the orders. In the Seller Portal's Orders tab, select the seller-fulfilled Marketplace orders you are processing. Print a pull sheet if you use one to pick the cards, and export the shipping information CSV.

2

Prepare the addresses. Upload the CSV to TCG Speed Shipper, review the names and addresses, and choose the output you need: Avery 5160, 4×6 address labels, envelopes, or packing slips.

3

Check the shipping tiers. Review which orders are grouped for envelopes, tracking, or signature confirmation. Use TCGplayer's current guidance and the carrier's rules when choosing the actual mail service.

4

Pack and pay for postage separately. For an envelope workflow, print the address or apply an address label and add postage. For a tracked package, buy postage from your carrier or postage platform and use its paid label.

5

Update the order. Keep the TCGplayer Order Number with the tracking number from your postage provider. Import the updated tracking file in the Seller Portal, or mark an eligible untracked order as shipped using the provided controls.

Common mix-ups to avoid

  • Calling an address label “postage.” A neat, correctly addressed envelope still needs postage.
  • Assuming a 4×6 printout is a carrier label. TCG Speed Shipper's 4×6 output is an address layout, not a prepaid label with a carrier barcode.
  • Uploading tracking without the order number. Keep the matching TCGplayer Order Number in the tracking file so the import can attach it to the right sale.
  • Shipping Direct orders one by one. Direct fulfillment means TCGplayer sends those buyer orders; sellers handle the later RI replenishment shipment instead.
  • Assuming every Marketplace parcel needs an insert. The Portal offers packing slips, but use the Direct RI packing-slip rule only for that separate replenishment workflow.

FAQ

Does TCGplayer provide postage labels for Marketplace orders?

No. TCGplayer's Marketplace help describes exporting order and shipping information, buying postage through a carrier or shipping platform, and uploading the resulting tracking data. TCG Speed Shipper makes address labels from the CSV, but it does not sell postage.

Are TCG Speed Shipper labels prepaid?

No. They are address labels or printed envelope layouts, not prepaid postage. Add stamps for an envelope when appropriate, or buy a carrier postage label separately for a tracked package.

Does every TCGplayer Marketplace package need a packing slip?

TCGplayer's Marketplace Seller Portal offers printable packing slips, but the Marketplace help pages reviewed here do not state that one must be included in every buyer package. Direct reimbursement shipments are different: their RI packing slip must be included.

How do I add tracking to a TCGplayer order?

Buy postage through your carrier or shipping platform, export the resulting tracking information with the TCGplayer Order Number, then upload the updated file from the Orders tab using Import Tracking. TCGplayer applies valid tracking numbers to the matching orders.

Does TCGplayer Pro ship orders for sellers?

TCGplayer describes Pro as a set of inventory, pricing, and store tools, and lists Direct and Store Your Products separately as fulfillment solutions. With Direct, TCGplayer ships eligible buyer orders and later sends the seller a Reimbursement Invoice.
