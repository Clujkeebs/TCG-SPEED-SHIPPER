TCGplayer Fees Explained

What TCGplayer takes from each sale, where the $0.30 hurts, and a calculator that shows your real payout before you list.

Published September 26, 2026 · 6 min read

TCGplayer's fees are simple once you see them written out, but they catch new sellers in two places: the commission is charged on shipping too, and the flat $0.30 per order eats a big share of small orders. Rates below are from TCGplayer's fee page as of September 26, 2026.

The short answer

If you're a Level 1-4 marketplace seller (most sellers are), each order costs:

  • Marketplace commission: 10.75% of the items and the shipping the buyer paid. The commission on items is capped at $75 per product sold; shipping has no cap.
  • Transaction fee: 2.5% of the order total + $0.30. The order total includes sales tax. International orders pay 3.5% + $0.30.

On a normal order that adds up to roughly 13.5-15% of what the buyer paid for cards and shipping. On a $1 card it's closer to 30%, because of the fixed $0.30.

How to keep more of each sale

  • Get buyers to combine orders. The $0.30 is charged per order, not per card, so five cards in one order pay it once.
  • Price shipping knowing it's taxed by the commission. 10.75% of the shipping goes to TCGplayer, so $0.99 shipping only brings you about $0.88 before postage.
  • Check your very cheap listings. A $0.25 card with $0.99 shipping pays $0.47 in fees and leaves $0.77 to cover a stamp, a sleeve and a toploader. Selling bulk as lots is often better.
  • Don't overspend on postage. The shipping method is the other big cost. How Much Does It Cost to Ship Trading Cards? compares stamps, Ground Advantage and tracked options, and TCGplayer's shipping guidelines say when tracking is required.

FAQ

What percentage does TCGplayer take?

For Level 1-4 marketplace sellers, 10.75% of the items and shipping, plus a transaction fee of 2.5% of the order total (including sales tax) and $0.30 per order. On a typical $20-$300 order that works out to about 13.5-15% of what the buyer paid for cards and shipping.

Does TCGplayer charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 10.75% commission applies to the shipping the buyer pays as well as the items, and the shipping has no cap.

Is there a cap on TCGplayer fees?

The commission on items is capped at $75 per product sold. The commission on shipping and the transaction fee are not capped.

Do TCGplayer sellers pay fees on sales tax?

Only through the transaction fee: the 2.5% is charged on the order total, which includes sales tax. The commission is charged on items and shipping only.

Is TCGplayer Pro cheaper?

Not on marketplace sales. Pro sellers pay 9.25% commission plus a 2.5% Pro fee, which is 11.75% compared with 10.75% for Level 1-4 sellers. Pro is paid for through its tools and website, not a lower marketplace rate.
