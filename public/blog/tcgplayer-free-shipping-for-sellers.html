TCGplayer Free Shipping for Sellers

Free shipping on TCGplayer isn't free on every order, and not every seller can offer it. Here's how the rules actually work — and the math on whether you should.

Published September 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Free shipping is one of the most-searched TCGplayer topics, and most of what you'll find is written for buyers. This one is for sellers: when you're allowed to offer it, how TCGplayer's $5 rule actually works, and whether it's worth doing on cards that sell for a quarter.

Who can offer free shipping

According to TCGplayer's seller help center (as of September 2026), Level 4 sellers can set their standard shipping rate to $0.00. Level 1–3 sellers have a set range for shipping prices — roughly $0.99 to $24.99 depending on product size — so $0 isn't available until you reach Level 4. If you're not there yet, TCGplayer Seller Levels Explained covers how to move up.

The $5 rule

Free shipping on TCGplayer isn't free on every order. Orders under $5 from a single seller get a $1.49 shipping fee applied automatically, whatever the seller set. For orders of $5 and up, your own shipping settings apply. So when a Level 4 seller sets standard shipping to $0, buyers see "free shipping on orders over $5" from that store.

That rule is what makes free shipping workable: you're never shipping a single 10¢ card for nothing.

How to set it up

1

Confirm you're Level 4 in the Seller Portal.

2

Open your shipping settings and set the standard shipping rate for the relevant product sizes to $0.00.

3

Save, then view your storefront as a buyer would to confirm the free-over-$5 message shows.

TCGplayer's help center has an article titled "How do I set up free shipping for orders over $5?" with the current screens. Settings pages move around, so use that if these steps don't match what you see.

Is it worth it?

Run the numbers on your typical order. A PWE costs you a stamp (plus the nonmachinable surcharge if it's rigid), a sleeve, a toploader, and an envelope. If most of your orders are $5–$15 in singles, that's a meaningful slice of the margin.

  • Why sellers do it: free shipping makes your listings more competitive when buyers compare the total cost, and it can win cart-optimizer matches against sellers charging a dollar or two.
  • Why sellers don't: on thin-margin singles, shipping is the margin. Some sellers raise prices slightly instead, which only works if your prices stay competitive.
  • The middle path: keep a modest shipping price and compete on speed and packaging instead. Buyers remember cards that arrive fast and flat.

Free shipping doesn't change the tracking rules

Offering free shipping doesn't change what TCGplayer requires: tracking at $49.99 and up, and signature confirmation at $250 and up. A free-shipping order over $50 still needs a tracked mailer — budget for it. The full rules are in TCGplayer Shipping Guidelines for Sellers.

FAQ

Who can offer free shipping on TCGplayer?

Per TCGplayer's help center, Level 4 sellers can set their standard shipping rate to $0. Levels 1–3 have a minimum shipping price range.

How does TCGplayer free shipping over $5 work?

Orders under $5 from a seller automatically get a $1.49 shipping fee. For orders of $5 and up, the seller's shipping settings apply, so a $0 rate shows as free shipping on orders over $5.

Does free shipping change TCGplayer's tracking requirements?

No. Orders of $49.99 or more still need tracking, and $250 or more still need signature confirmation.
