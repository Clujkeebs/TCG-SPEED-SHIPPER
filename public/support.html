Printing & Labels

My CSV won't load, or says it can't find the name/address columns

Use TCGplayer's order/shipping export: the file with one row per order, including buyer name and address. The pull sheet lists cards but no addresses, so it can't make labels. If you edited the file in Excel, save it as CSV (Comma delimited). Still stuck? Re-export it — the second export is often clean. More detail is in the CSV format guide.

Labels print too small, too big, or off-center

In the print dialog, set scale to 100% / "Actual size", not "Fit to page." For 4×6 thermal printers, also set the paper size to 4×6 in and margins to none. For Avery 5160 sheets, print one test page on plain paper first and hold it behind a label sheet against a light to check alignment.

Some characters on a label look wrong or show "?"

PDF labels use standard fonts that cover English and Western European letters. Accented letters outside that set are simplified (for example "ễ" becomes "e"), and scripts like Japanese or Cyrillic show as "?". For those orders, handwrite the address or copy it from TCGplayer directly.

Does this buy postage or create tracking numbers?

No. TCG Speed Shipper prints address labels, envelopes, and packing slips. Buy postage from USPS (stamps, Click-N-Ship) or a service like Pirate Ship. Afterwards you can paste those tracking numbers into the Tracking Numbers panel for a quick-copy list to enter back into TCGplayer.

I re-downloaded the same batch. Did that use more labels?

No. Once a batch is counted, re-downloading the same orders in another format — or after a printer jam — is free until you load a different file.

Account & Sign-In

I forgot my password

On the Pricing page, enter your email and click Forgot your password?. The reset email can take a few minutes and sometimes lands in spam or promotions. Clicking the link signs you in and asks you to choose a new password. If it never arrives, email us and we'll sort it out by hand.

It says an account with my email already exists

Click Log In instead, or use Forgot your password?. If you signed up long ago with an emailed sign-in link and never set a password, clicking Create Account with that email sets one for you.

How do I change my password?

Sign in, open the Pricing page, and click Change Password.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Email us from your account's address and we'll delete it, usually within a couple of days. Cancel any active subscription first from Manage Billing, or just ask and we'll handle both. Settings saved in your browser (return address, preferences) can be cleared anytime by clearing this site's data in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Billing

How do I cancel?

Sign in, go to the Pricing page, and click Manage Billing, then Cancel plan. You keep your paid features until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. Or email us and we'll cancel it for you.

I paid but my account still says Free

Refresh the page once. Upgrades usually show within seconds, and the site double-checks with Stripe automatically if it's slow. Make sure you're signed in with the same email you used at checkout. If it still says Free after a few minutes, email us — you won't be charged twice, and we'll fix it the same day.

Can I get a refund?

If you were charged by mistake, charged after trying to cancel, or it just didn't work for you, email us within 14 days of the charge and we'll make it right. See the refund policy.

How do I switch between Base and Premium?

Click the other plan's upgrade button on the Pricing page. Your existing subscription is changed, not duplicated, and Stripe credits the unused part of your old plan.

How does "refer a friend" work?

Share the link from your Account panel. When someone signs up through it and becomes a paying customer, you get a free month: a billing credit if you're subscribed, or 30 days of free Premium if you're not.
