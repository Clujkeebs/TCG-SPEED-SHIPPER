The fine print

What counts as a referral?

Someone who first visits through your link and creates an account from that browser. Commission is earned on their real payments (after any free month and discounts), for as long as they stay subscribed and the program runs.

Do I have to disclose it?

Yes. The FTC requires a clear disclosure whenever you're paid for a recommendation, for example "I earn a commission if you sign up through my link." Say it in the video or post itself, not only in a description people won't read.

What isn't allowed?

Referring yourself or your own accounts, spam or unsolicited bulk messages, bidding on our brand name in search ads, or misleading claims (for example saying it buys postage — it doesn't; it makes the labels). Commission from abuse is reversed. See the Terms of Service.

Can the program change?

We may change or end the program for the future with notice by email. Commission you've already earned stays yours and is paid out.
